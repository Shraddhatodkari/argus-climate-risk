[SECTION: Purpose]
The framework provides a common baseline for voluntary disclosure of climate-related
financial risks by internationally active banks, intended to complement (not replace)
jurisdiction-specific disclosure requirements such as those issued by national supervisors.

[SECTION: Physical Risk Disclosure]
Banks are encouraged to disclose quantitative exposure metrics for physical risk, including
the geographic distribution of credit exposures in areas subject to elevated acute hazard
(flood, cyclone, wildfire) and chronic hazard (drought, heat stress, sea-level rise), using
the most granular geographic level practicable.

[SECTION: Transition Risk Disclosure]
Banks are encouraged to disclose exposure to carbon-intensive sectors and the potential
impact of policy, technology, and market shifts associated with the transition to a
lower-carbon economy on asset quality and capital adequacy.

[SECTION: Data and Methodology Caveats]
Given the evolving state of climate data and methodologies, banks are encouraged to disclose
the data sources, assumptions, and limitations underlying their climate risk metrics, and to
update methodologies as better data becomes available.
