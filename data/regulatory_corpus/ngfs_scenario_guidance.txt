[SECTION: Scenario Families]
NGFS scenarios are commonly grouped into Orderly (early, coordinated climate policy action),
Disorderly (late or divergent policy action), and Hot House World (limited or no additional
policy action, resulting in higher physical risk realization).

[SECTION: Use in Financial Risk Assessment]
Financial institutions are encouraged to use NGFS scenarios as a common reference point for
climate stress-testing and scenario analysis, translating macro-financial variables from the
scenarios into portfolio-level physical and transition risk impacts.

[SECTION: Physical Risk Modeling Guidance]
Physical risk modeling should account for both acute hazards (event-driven, e.g. flood or
cyclone damage) and chronic hazards (gradual, e.g. changing precipitation or temperature
patterns), and should be updated as hazard datasets and climate projections improve.
