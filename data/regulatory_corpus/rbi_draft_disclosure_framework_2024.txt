[SECTION: Governance]
Regulated entities are expected to disclose the role of their Board and senior management
in overseeing and managing climate-related risks and opportunities, including how climate
considerations are factored into business strategy, risk appetite, and major transactions.

[SECTION: Strategy]
Entities should describe the climate-related risks and opportunities identified over the
short, medium, and long term, and the actual and potential impacts on the business model,
strategy, and financial planning. Where feasible, entities should describe the resilience
of their strategy under different climate-related scenarios, including a scenario aligned
with limiting global warming.

[SECTION: Risk Management]
Entities should disclose how climate-related risks are identified, assessed, and managed,
and how these processes are integrated into the entity's overall risk management framework.
Physical risks (acute, such as floods and cyclones; and chronic, such as shifting rainfall
patterns) and transition risks (policy, legal, technology, market, and reputation) should
both be addressed.

[SECTION: Metrics and Targets]
Entities should disclose the metrics used to assess climate-related risks and opportunities,
including exposure to physical risk by geography or sector where material, along with any
targets used to manage climate-related risks and performance against those targets.

[SECTION: Phased Applicability]
The framework is proposed to apply in a phased manner to Scheduled Commercial Banks, and
subsequently to NBFCs and other regulated entities above defined thresholds, with disclosures
expected on a comply-or-explain basis initially.
